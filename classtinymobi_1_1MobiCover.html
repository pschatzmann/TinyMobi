Detailed Description

template<typename RGB_T = TINYMD_DEFAULT_RGB_T, typename Doc = MemoryMobi>
class tinymobi::MobiCover< RGB_T, Doc >

The cover of a book. load() decodes the JPEG cover of the open document into a sprite scaled to the largest size that fits the screen; view is the widget that shows it. Covers in other formats are not shown.
