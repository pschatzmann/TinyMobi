Limitations

Back to the README

  • DRM: reported as Error::Encrypted.
  • The FDST flow table is not parsed. The KF8 text is assumed to start at offset 0, which holds for the books tested.
  • The repair path for malformed KF8 fragment positions (KindleUnpack's fallback) is not ported. Badly formed KF8 books may assemble differently.
  • Links are resolved from the link text to the position of its target. The KF8 links (kindle:pos) are mapped through the fragment table, which was checked against the text of the books tested. Links that name a place by its text (cross-references) can land a few lines away from the words of the link.
  • Reading a page of a KF8 book starts from the beginning of its part, so turning pages takes longer in long parts. MOBI6 books are not affected.
  • TocEntry::start (the NCX index) is only resolved for MOBI6 books; for KF8 books its section is kNoRecord. Use the links in the text instead.
  • Images are not decoded. The bytes go to a decoder library of your choice.
  • Hybrid books are read through their KF8 text, not their MOBI6 text.
